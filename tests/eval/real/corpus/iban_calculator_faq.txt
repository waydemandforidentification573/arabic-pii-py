Q: What does a valid IBAN look like in my country?
A: Below are the standard worked examples published in the public IBAN
   registry for each GCC member state. Use them only to check the shape
   and length of your own IBAN — they are documentation samples.

Saudi Arabia (SA, 24 chars): SA4420000001234567891234
   Printed grouping: SA44 2000 0001 2345 6789 1234

United Arab Emirates (AE, 23 chars): AE460090000000123456789

Qatar (QA, 29 chars): QA54QNBA000000000000693123456

Kuwait (KW, 30 chars): KW81CBKU0000000000001234560101

Bahrain (BH, 22 chars): BH02CITI00001077181611

Oman (OM, 23 chars): OM040280000012345678901

Q: ما هو نموذج الآيبان المعتمد لدى البنك المركزي السعودي؟
A: المثال الرسمي المنشور في لائحة مؤسسة النقد (ساما) هو:
   الصيغة المطبوعة: SA39 1500 0999 1031 4343 0001
   الصيغة الإلكترونية: SA3915000999103143430001

Q: Do these examples belong to a real customer?
A: No. They are reference samples only and must never be used to send
   money. Always obtain your own IBAN from your bank statement.
