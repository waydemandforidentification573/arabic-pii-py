ZATCA / FATOORA — Simplified Tax Invoice (worked example)
فاتورة ضريبية مبسطة — مثال هيئة الزكاة والضريبة والجمارك
======================================================================
This excerpt reproduces the seller fields from the published ZATCA
QR-code worked example used in the developer guidance.

Seller's name: Bobs Records
VAT registration number of the seller: 310122393500003
Time stamp of the invoice (date and time): 2022-04-25T15:30:00Z
Invoice total (with VAT): 1000.00 SAR
VAT total: 150.00 SAR

A second worked example in the same guidance uses the seller:
Seller's name: Bobs Basement Records
VAT registration number: 100025906700003
Time stamp: 2022-04-25T15:30:00Z

Note: every Saudi tax invoice must carry the supplier's 15-digit VAT
number. For Saudi-established sellers the number begins with the GCC
member-state digit 3.
