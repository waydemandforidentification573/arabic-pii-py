Travel Document Specimen — ICAO Doc 9303 worked example
نموذج وثيقة سفر — المثال المعتمد في وثيقة الإيكاو 9303
======================================================================
The ICAO Doc 9303 standard publishes a specimen passport for the
fictional state of Utopia (country code UTO) to illustrate the layout
of a machine-readable travel document.

Holder (VIZ): ERIKSSON, ANNA MARIA
Nationality: Utopia (UTO)
Date of birth: 12 AUG 1974
Document number: L898902C3

Machine-readable zone (MRZ):
P<UTOERIKSSON<<ANNA<MARIA<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<
L898902C36UTO7408122F3404159ZE184226B<<<<<16

This specimen is published purely as documentation; the holder is a
fictional person and the document number is an illustrative example.
